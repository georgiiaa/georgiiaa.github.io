Georgia Todd Barrett

I am interested in technology, philosophy, theology, linguistics, hermeneutics, ontology, temporality, and the ethics of machine intelligence.

My scholastic background is interdisciplinary. I studied computer science and philosophy at NYU.

I currently work in product at an AI company that develops voice agents.

My loves: C.S. Lewis (particularly his theological works, Letters to Malcolm and God in the Dock), Kierkegaard, Yves Klein, my dogs Elvis and Eleanor, mixed martial arts, aviation, etymology, walking, cinematography, Prague, notetaking, and my family.
