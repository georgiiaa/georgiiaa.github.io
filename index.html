About

I am an interdisciplinary thinker with a passion for charting paths through disciplines that seem unrelated — until they aren't.

My background spans philosophy, computer science, and the humanities, rooted in my time at NYU's Gallatin School of Individualized Study. I write about technology, ethics, and ideas on Substack. I care deeply about ethical AI, the nature of meaning, and the hidden architecture connecting seemingly separate fields.

When I'm not writing or thinking, I'm training in kickboxing and MMA, watching cinematography that moves me, or losing myself in a good science fiction novel. Everything has dimensions. I'm interested in all of them.
